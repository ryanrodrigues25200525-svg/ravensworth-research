Do your own work. Consult a licensed professional before acting on anything you
read here.

## Hypothetical performance

The [model portfolio]({{ '/portfolio/' | relative_url }}) is hypothetical and
unfunded. No capital is at risk and no orders are routed to any venue.

Positions are recorded at the closing price on the publication date of the
corresponding report. Results exclude commissions, slippage, market impact,
short-borrow costs, financing, dividends and taxes. Marks are last trade or
previous close from public sources and are not executable prices. Actual results
achieved with real capital would differ and would generally be worse.

Hypothetical results carry the inherent limitation of being constructed with the
benefit of hindsight over the period shown, and are not indicative of future
performance.

Ravensworth Research holds no positions in any security mentioned. The model

## Data and accuracy

Content is compiled from public sources believed reliable, including SEC filings,
earnings transcripts, market data vendors and official statistical agencies.
Accuracy and completeness are not guaranteed. Figures are as of the timestamp
stated in each report and are not updated as facts change, except that portfolio
marks are refreshed automatically.

Reports are generated by a large language model. Reasoning errors, mis-extracted
figures and stale data are possible. Verify any figure at its source before
relying on it.
